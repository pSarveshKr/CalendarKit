Privacy Policy

Extension: CalendarKit

Last updated: May 2026

Data Collection

CalendarKit does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

Local Storage

The extension stores the following data locally on your device using Chrome's storage API:

  • Your Google Calendar embed URL
  • Calculator history
  • Your default tab preference

This data never leaves your device.

Third Party Services

CalendarKit embeds Google Calendar via an iframe. Google's own Privacy Policy applies to your calendar data.

Permissions

  • storage — To save your preferences locally
  • tabs — To open Google Calendar in a new tab
  • alarms — To update the extension icon daily
